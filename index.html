Core Sentence Structure & Particles

Japanese basic word order is SOV (Subject - Object - Verb). Particles mark the role of each word.

は (wa)

Topic Marker

Written as 'ha' but pronounced 'wa'. It indicates the topic of the sentence ("As for...").

わたし は たの です。(Watashi wa Thanu desu.)
I am Thanu.
を (o / wo)

Direct Object Marker

Indicates the object that is directly receiving the action of a verb.

ほん を よみます。(Hon o yomimasu.)
I read a book.
に (ni)

Time & Destination Marker

Marks a specific point in time or direction towards a goal/place.

にほん に いきます。(Nihon ni ikimasu.)
I am going to Japan.
で (de)

Location of Action & Means

Specifies where an action takes place or the instrument/tool used.

としょかん で べんきょうします。(Toshokan de benkyoushimasu.)
I study at the library.
